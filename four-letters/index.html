    Sheet · forty words

    Words are built from the Gazetteer’s inventory: eleven vowels, seventeen single onsets and twenty clusters, and its word-final codas. Where two consonants meet across a syllable break, only pairings that read well are allowed. Sonority falls into the break (l·t, r·d), a nasal takes the place of the stop after it (n·d, m·b), s leads a stop (s·t), or a stop leans on a liquid (t·r). Coined words are checked against a dictionary, so none of them is accidentally real.
