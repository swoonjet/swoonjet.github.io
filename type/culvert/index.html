Notes

All capitals, on both cases of the keyboard: plain keys set Solid, Shift sets Inline (or Relief in the Relief styles). Figures and basic punctuation, no accents. Culvert Waterways is the map kit: 26 pieces on keys a to z, of which 18 connect on a shared grid when typed, with no tracking or leading changes, and 8 are standalone symbols. Utypeia moved the Waterways pieces onto that grid; nothing was redrawn. The six text styles keep the designer's outlines, spacing and kerning.
