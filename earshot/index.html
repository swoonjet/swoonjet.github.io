Open microphones, permanently installed in places around the world, are streaming right now. This piece listens to several at once — chosen to be as far apart as possible — and composes from whatever happens to coincide. Everything you hear is arriving live. Nothing was recorded in advance.

          Mixer

          Faders move a place as a whole — the live stream and the layers the piece generates from it. None of this touches the analysis: pull one to nothing and the spectrogram, the pattern matching and the narration carry on. Level matching sits upstream of the faders: over a few seconds it lifts the quiet places a long way and trims the loud ones a little, toward a common level. Each strip shows what it is doing, in dB. Turn it off to hear the set as it really arrives.

              Starting

              Everything below advances on its own — the microphone list is fetched, the furthest-apart set is chosen, and each stream is opened as it is reached. There is nothing to configure and nothing to click through.
