Trungle Bunch

        

          Triangular fruiting bodies on a living network. Three tuned edges each.
          Threads grow toward consonance and rot where they are unused.
        

        

          
click the soil
germinate a triangle

          
tap a triangle
strike the edge the field points at

          
drag its middle
carry it — height sets its register

          
drag a corner
pull its shape about — this retunes its voice

          
drag its rim
turn it, bending the note it is sounding

          
shove one into another
they thud — the bunch has drums in it

          
keep playing one
it tempers, and its bell squares up

          
hover a cluster
it shivers and tunes itself to its neighbours

          
sweep sideways
bend time — the network's metabolism

          
sweep up and down
bend gravity — rooted or ascendant

          
wheel over an edge
nudge that edge into microtonal space

          
wheel over the soil
push a body through depth

          
double-click
dissolve a triangle

          
space
let the colony fall quiet

          
esc
back to this screen — the colony keeps growing behind it
