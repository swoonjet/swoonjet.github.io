Selected work 2026

Project Field
  1. EarshotFour live field mics, thousands of kilometres apart, streaming right now. Nothing is pre-recorded — every layer comes from whatever happens to coincide. Live audio · Composition →
  2. HeliosphereLive solar wind and geomagnetic data, translated into sound. The sun, audible. NOAA’s feed from 1.5 million kilometres out tunes a stack of drones: wind speed sets the pitch, the magnetic field pulls it out of tune, and a geomagnetic storm pushes it into distortion. Sonification →
  3. CadastreGenerate a whole city from a seed — terrain, streets, parcels, parks, every place named. Pan, zoom, export it poster-size. Generative · Cartography →
  4. LingerShort fiction and poetry at the speed of reflection. No scrolling, no skimming — just the prose. Reading →
  5. Weather ReportsForty-six writers, one premise: an unexplained weather phenomenon over World C6H-ɛ. Read it as anthology, archive, or apply for a researcher post. Anthology · Fiction →
  6. Pegbelt Shape HouseShapes made of a belt pulled tight round pegs on a grid: lobes, V-bites and hairline necks. Generate one, set it moving like machinery, and take it away as SVG, PNG, pegs or video. Generative · Shape →
  7. GrainfieldA browser-based granular synthesis explorer. Pull a sound apart on a node canvas and listen to its grains. Synthesis →
  8. Type DesignTen original typefaces, from a razor-edged Didone and a stocky Art Deco cut to an insect-legged display face and a modular kit that types maps. Each gets its own specimen to try; all are free on Utypeia. Typefaces →
  9. Chord LoomBuild a chord and load it onto one of twelve breathing pads, each with its own envelope, tape-loop memory, and reverb. Instrument · Web Audio →
  10. Single Cell BattleA turn-based microorganism battler ported from a Python sim. Roll a roster, fight the computer's, win and evolve — lose three times and die. Game · Roguelike →
  11. MoiraA base curve swept through hundreds of rotations until it interferes with itself. Nine variations on an old moiré sketch, with inks that actually mix. Generative →
  12. JemsRandom points, Delaunay-triangulated into a mosaic of tonal glass. Regenerate for a new arrangement, export as SVG or PNG. Generative →
  13. LampoTwelve random points in four dimensions, resolved into a Bezier surface of revolution. Tune it, orbit it, keep the good ones. Generative →
  14. Lateral WalksA trained lateral thinker looks through live webcams and follows the chain of ideas wherever it goes. Agent · Webcams →
  15. StreetwanderAn agent takes creative walks through real neighborhoods — notices briefly, thinks a lot. Each walk leaves a main artifact and a few companions. Agent · Streetview →
  16. CuriothymiaA mind that wanders. Threads of thought, branching by association. Let your attention follow. Agent · Mind →
  17. Dual-EmergenceTwo opposed agents negotiate every stage of a planet's life, formation to deep future. Eight worlds; some plateau, some endure. Agent · Worlds →
  18. Musical MorphospaceA curiosity-driven agent exploring the space of possible musical grammars — the musics humanity didn't happen to invent. Agent · Music →
  19. AkrosA complete constructed language built from scratch by autonomous AI agents. 735 words, 99 grammar patterns, a 7-god pantheon. Language · Agent →
  20. MarmaladeAn autonomous generative collage engine making art in the wild, on its own schedule. Generative →
  21. Stimviz138 digital archives spanning illustration, design, audio, architecture, typography, science, photography, and film. Archive →
  22. TerragodRoll a continent, an island, an atoll, or a whole world. Pan, zoom, export as SVG or PNG. Generative · Maps →
  23. GazetteerInvent place names from a phoneme grammar. Seed one by integer, coordinate, or landmark, and watch it break apart. Generative · Toponymy →
  24. Trungle BunchA generative instrument grown, not laid out. Triangular bodies on a mycelial network, voiced by the colours their threads join. Instrument · Web Audio →
  25. PetriTwo views of the same organism. One tab lets a colony crawl, eat, and grow under a cursor's pull; the other pins a single specimen to a slide — cell wall, nucleus, and reach rendered entirely in dots and lines. Generative · Specimen →
  26. MagpieFive magpies out looking for interesting things, each hunting a different way — by theme, by era, by obscurity, by memory. What they carry home gets transformed and woven into the collage. Agent · Sound →
  27. Seismic EntropyTrue random numbers pulled from live seismic sensor noise at nine stations worldwide — a wall of raw integers, each traceable to real ground motion happening right now. Live data · Randomness →
  28. SFRC-RADIOAn unattended internet radio station curated by an agent reading live planetary data — geomagnetic activity, solar wind, weather, aircraft overhead, the sun's position — and letting the sky decide what plays next. Radio · Agent curator →
  29. Pangaea OtherwiseA speculative plate-tectonics generator. It starts from the real Pangaea at 250 million years ago, lets you change how the supercontinent breaks apart and how the pieces drift, then plays out alternate Earths, and runs the clock on into the future. Generative · Tectonics →
