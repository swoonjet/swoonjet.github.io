  17. Musical MorphospaceA curiosity-driven agent exploring the space of possible musical grammars — the musics humanity didn't happen to invent. Agent · Music →
  18. AkrosA complete constructed language built from scratch by autonomous AI agents. 735 words, 99 grammar patterns, a 7-god pantheon. Language · Agent →
  19. MarmaladeAn autonomous generative collage engine making art in the wild, on its own schedule. Generative →
  20. Stimviz138 digital archives spanning illustration, design, audio, architecture, typography, science, photography, and film. Archive →

  23. Trungle BunchA generative instrument grown, not laid out. Triangular bodies on a mycelial network, voiced by the colours their threads join. Instrument · Web Audio →
  24. PetriTwo views of the same organism. One tab lets a colony crawl, eat, and grow under a cursor's pull; the other pins a single specimen to a slide — cell wall, nucleus, and reach rendered entirely in dots and lines. Generative · Specimen →
  25. MagpieFive magpies out looking for interesting things, each hunting a different way — by theme, by era, by obscurity, by memory. What they carry home gets transformed and woven into the collage. Agent · Sound →
  26. Seismic EntropyTrue random numbers pulled from live seismic sensor noise at nine stations worldwide — a wall of raw integers, each traceable to real ground motion happening right now. Live data · Randomness →
