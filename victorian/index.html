Old Mine Cut Diamond Solitaire Ring

15ct Yellow Gold, English, circa 1875
CAD $4,800
Details
Era Victorian (1837–1901)
Date circa 1875
Metal 15ct Yellow Gold
Stone Diamond, Old Mine Cut
Carat Weight Approx. 0.85ct
Ring Size 6.5 (resizable)
Hallmarks 15ct, English assay, legible
Condition Excellent
Origin England
Stock No. ARD-2024-1847

A beautifully preserved Victorian solitaire, set with an old mine cut diamond in a classic four-claw collet mount. The stone carries the characteristic warmth and depth of the period — gently rounded girdle, high crown, small table — and catches light in the way modern cuts simply don't replicate.


The shank is a clean, slender 15ct gold band with subtle shoulder taper. Hallmarks are legible and consistent with an English origin, circa 1875. The ring has been professionally cleaned and is in excellent overall condition.

Provenance
Acquired from a private Ontario collection. Previously held by a single family for three generations; no prior retail history.
