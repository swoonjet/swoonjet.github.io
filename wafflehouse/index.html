Waffle House — Judging Criteria

Each story must involve a waffle house, or the idea of a waffle house.

1 · Shortlisting 2 · Scoring Matrix Score Calculator 3 · Tiebreakers Downloads

1Shortlisting

Narrow the entries to a manageable shortlist before anyone does detailed scoring.

  • Two reads per story. Every story is seen by at least two judges, not one — so one judge's off-day can't sink a good entry.
  • Simple gate here. Each reader marks Advance / Maybe / Cut + one sentence why.
  • Resolve: two Advance → in · two Cut → out · any split → quick group look.
  • Target shortlist: 8–10 stories. Then all judges score that set with the matrix below.

2The Scoring Matrix

Shortlisted stories only. Each criterion scored 1–100, multiplied by its weight. Weights sum to 100%.

#CriterionWhat we're rewardingWeight
1Craft / Writing QualityProse, control of language, structure, pacing, an ending that lands35%
2Idea & OriginalityFreshness of concept, surprise, a take we haven't seen ten times30%
3Brief Fit — Waffle House gateHow meaningfully the waffle house (literal or as idea/metaphor) is woven in — not just name-dropped20%
4Overall ImpactThe gut score: did it move you, stay with you, would you publish it?15%

Weighted total = (Craft×.35) + (Idea×.30) + (Brief×.20) + (Impact×.15) → out of 100

  • Judges only enter a 1–100 per criterion. Each judge's scorecard works out that judge's weighted total automatically.
  • Final score per story = the average of the judges' weighted totals, computed in the separate master tally sheet.
  • Brief Fit is a gate: any story scoring 30 or below on criterion 3 is disqualified, whatever its total.

3Tiebreakers

If two stories finish with the same final score, work down this list until the tie breaks:

  1. Higher Craft score — reward the better-written piece.
  2. Higher Idea & Originality score — then the more original.
  3. Ranked head-to-head vote. Each judge ranks the tied stories; lowest total ranking wins. A ranked vote can itself end in a tie — in that case the lead/editor casts the deciding vote.
